# Whop Pilot: Mission Control & AI Mentor Assistant 🚀

Whop Pilot transforms your shop from a static course into a high-performance creator ecosystem. It acts as your 24/7 "Mission Control," analyzing all signals from your store to provide you with daily, action-oriented insights.

## 🔥 Key Features

### 🛠️ Daily Mission Control
No more drowning in data. Whop Pilot summarizes forum posts, student progress, and payment statuses to prepare a 3-item "Daily Mission" list that tells you exactly what you need to do every morning.

### 🤖 "Hocam" Proactive AI Mentor
Your next-generation AI mentor has specialized in community management. It no longer just "generates text"; it analyzes silent students, users who left at checkout, and "hot-spot" issues within the community to offer proactive suggestions: *"Hocam, students seem stuck on Chapter 2, let's post an update."*

### 💸 Revenue Recovery Engine
Instantly identify those who left the shop at the payment step or students who started the course and stopped. Whop Pilot simulates the amount of "Recoverable Revenue" gaining these users back would bring you and offers the tools needed to win them back.

### 📊 Real-Time Community Pulse
See the "vibe" of your community instantly. Track student completion rates and forum interaction speeds right from your dashboard.

---

**Fully Compatible with Whop v2 SDK.** Deeply integrated into the Whop infrastructure, Pilot offers native speed and reliability from forums to payments.
